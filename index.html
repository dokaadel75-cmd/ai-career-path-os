Stage 1 · apply selectively now

Instructional Designer / Learning Content Designer

Why you fit: teaching, curriculum content, question design, presentation development, online teaching, and training experience.

Hiring skills:
  • Needs analysis, objectives, ADDIE/SAM basics
  • Assessment, Bloom, adult learning
  • SME collaboration and content accuracy
  • Learning materials, storyboards, facilitation
  • AI-assisted development with quality review
Gap to close: formal end-to-end case study; assessment rationale; adult/corporate learning framing; documented outcomes.
Stage 2 · main target

Learning Experience Designer (LXD)

Hiring skills: learner discovery/personas, learning journeys, UX/accessibility, prototyping, instructional strategies, facilitation, stakeholder management, evaluation/data, authoring tools and LMS familiarity.

Gap to close: Storyline/Rise hands-on sample, learner research, usability feedback, metrics, polished case study, collaboration process.
Stage 3 · specialization

AI Learning Experience Designer / AI Learning Specialist

Hiring skills: AI concepts and tools, prompt design, AI-enabled learning journeys, practical labs/scenarios, evaluation, responsible AI, privacy/bias, AI prototyping, workflow integration.

Gap to close: teach AI with a measurable workshop; develop AI lab and prompt/evaluation rubric; document safe use and performance impact.
Stage 3 · parallel

AI Trainer / AI Training Specialist

Hiring skills: facilitation, presentation, audience adaptation, practical business workflows, training materials, adult learning, AI tool fluency, responsible use, feedback and evaluation.

Gap to close: recorded demo, at least 2–3 delivered workshops, attendee feedback, trainer guide, assessment and impact evidence.
Stage 4 · longer-term target

AI Enablement / Adoption Specialist

Hiring skills: discovery, stakeholder communication, workflow analysis, role-based use cases, change management, training, responsible AI governance, metrics and adoption support.

Gap to close: workplace AI adoption pilot, before/after workflow map, stakeholder interviews, adoption metrics, support assets and lessons learned.
Stage 4 · specialist / portfolio-led

AI Workflow Designer / Learning Automation Builder

Hiring skills: process mapping, n8n/Make, APIs and data basics, prompt/structured output, error handling, human approval, privacy and documentation.

Gap to close: 2 reliable demos, workflow diagrams, failure cases, test plan and clear business value.
Community pathway · opportunity based

AI Educator / Community Educator / Ambassador

Evidence: consistent educational posts, open workshops, community contributions, projects, volunteer facilitation, public speaking and collaboration. These programs are selective and availability changes; treat as opportunity pathway, not guaranteed employment.

Portfolio: evidence that makes you hireable

1 · AI-Powered Lesson Design

Problem, learner profile, objectives, lesson flow, AI prompts, critique, human revisions, assessment alignment.

Proof: case study + prompt log + final lesson.

2 · AI Assessment Design

Question bank with Bloom mapping, distractor rationale, quality rubric, bias/accuracy review.

Proof: sample bank + evaluation sheet.

3 · AI Fluency Workshop

60–90 minute workshop with objectives, agenda, demo, hands-on activity, facilitator guide, quiz and feedback.

Proof: delivered session, recording, participant feedback.

4 · Interactive E-learning

10–15 min module in Storyline/Rise, scenario, interaction, feedback, accessibility, publish/test.

Proof: demo + source outline + QA report.

5 · Learning Analytics

Use sample or anonymized learning data to diagnose completion/performance and recommend interventions.

Proof: dashboard + insight memo.

6 · Educational AI Automation

Workflow for content-to-question extraction/tagging/review with approval gate and error handling.

Proof: live demo + diagram + test cases.

Role-based learning program + tool matrix + prompt library + workflow + AI knowledge assistant prototype (optional advanced) + workshop + assessment + evaluation + 3–5 minute demo video.
